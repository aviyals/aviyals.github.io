Weather

Learn useful words and phrases for talking about the weather in everyday conversations.

Vocabulary

Sunny
The sun is shining. "It's sunny today."
Cloudy
The sky is covered with clouds. "It's cloudy this morning."
Rainy
Rain is falling. "It's a rainy day, so take an umbrella."
Windy
There is a lot of wind. "It's windy outside."
Chilly
A little cold. "It's chilly this evening."

Useful phrases

  • What's the weather like today?
  • It looks like it might rain.
  • It's getting warmer.
  • What's the forecast for tomorrow?
  • The weather was good at the start of the day, then a sudden downpour ruined our afternoon plans.
  • The weather was good at the start of the day, then the wind picked up and it started to pour.
  • Fair weather was forecast for the following day.
  • The weather is expected to remain clear for the next few days.
  • The match has been cancelled due to adverse weather conditions.
  • The rescue operation has been complicated by bad weather.

Example conversation

Alex: What's the weather like outside?

Sam: It's sunny, but a little chilly.

Alex: Should I take a jacket?

Sam: Yes, it might get colder this afternoon.

Practice

Describe today's weather in your town using two or three sentences. Then say what you will wear or take with you.
